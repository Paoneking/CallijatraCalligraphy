Callijatra Calligraphy

Privacy Policy

Callijatra Foundation · Effective 30 September 2026

Callijatra Calligraphy does not collect your drawings, your writing or any personal data.

There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. What you draw stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself.

The one thing that ever leaves the app is an error report, sent to Google’s Firebase Crashlytics when the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, so the fault can be found and fixed.

What the app stores on your device

WhatWhyWhere
Your canvases, strokes and layersSo your work is there when you come backThe app’s private storage
Pictures you import to trace overTo show them under your inkThe app’s private storage
Practice progress and stroke orders you recordTo remember what you have practisedThe app’s private storage
Settings, themes, brush tuning, recent coloursTo remember how you set the app upThe app’s private storage

None of this is transmitted, and none of it is readable by us.

You can erase it at any time by deleting canvases in the app, by clearing the app’s data in Android settings, or by uninstalling. Removing the app removes everything it stored.

Exporting and sharing

When you export a drawing, the app saves the file to your gallery or to a folder on your device that your file manager can open, or hands it to the app you choose in Android’s share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and that app. Callijatra Calligraphy does not upload it anywhere.

If the app crashes or hits an error

When the released app crashes, or runs into an error it did not expect, it sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so the fault can be diagnosed. A report contains:

  • the error and a stack trace showing where in the app it happened;
  • the app version, the device model, the Android version and the device’s state at the moment of the crash (for example, free memory and orientation);
  • a random installation identifier created by Firebase, which is not linked to your name, email address or any account.

A report never contains your drawings, anything you have written, pictures you imported or your contacts. The app does not attach a user identifier or any custom data to a report. Google keeps Crashlytics reports for 90 days. Development builds report nothing.

Permissions, and why each exists

  • Storage, Android 9 and older only (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — to save an exported drawing to your gallery. Newer versions of Android do not need it and the app does not ask for it there.
  • Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used only by Crashlytics, to send error reports and fetch its own settings. Everything you do in the app works fully offline.

To trace over a picture you choose it in Android’s own photo picker, which hands the app only that one picture. The app has no permission to read your photo library.

The app does not request photos, location, camera, microphone, contacts or phone permissions.

Links

A few pages in the app, such as About, link to websites. Those open in your browser and are covered by that website’s own policy.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of any age. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
